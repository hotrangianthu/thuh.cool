4CONSENSUS READING VS OPERATOR REALITY

The Consensus Reading

Vietnam is on a structural rise — China+1 is a decade-long supply chain shift, not a cycle trade
Government signals are strong — special economic zones, FDI law reform, and diplomatic positioning
ASEAN+ intra-regional trade is accelerating, compounding Vietnam's gateway position for Southeast Asia

The Operator Reality

60% froze investment — Vietnam's gain is within a shrinking active-capital pool. This is a fight for a smaller pie, not an expanding one.
Concentration ≠ breadth — FDI intent is corridor-concentrated. Most of Vietnam's territory and SME base sits outside the investment zone.
Intent ≠ disbursement — registered FDI historically converts at 60–70%. The 15% headline overstates actual capital arrival. [NEEDS SIZING]
Vietnam hasn't captured growth capital — it has captured resilience capital. The difference defines how long this lasts and what infrastructure it demands.

6OPERATOR INSIGHT

The B2B Gap Nobody Is Pricing: The 15% intent figure represents hundreds of manufacturing, logistics, and supply chain firms actively evaluating Vietnam. Every one of them needs treasury, payroll, compliance, and cross-border payment infrastructure in VND on day one — and most will find the product stack years behind consumer fintech maturity. For platform operators building in Vietnam, the incoming FDI wave is a B2B market signal more than a consumer demand signal.
