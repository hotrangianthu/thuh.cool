The Transfer Narrative
Budget transfers reach 47 provinces — fiscal redistribution is working by design
Infrastructure spend approved centrally, allocated to all regions annually
Northern highlands receive 85–95% of budgets as transfers from richer provinces
The Capacity Reality
40% of capex sits stranded — provinces lack procurement and execution capacity to spend it
No digital procurement backbone — contractor discovery and payment happen on paper
Trapped provinces atrophy further — weak infra → low FDI → weak tax base → repeat

"Vietnam's fiscal transfers redistribute money efficiently — but redistribute capacity not at all."

Operator View

Stranded capex is stranded working capital. Contractors can't bid without financing; provinces can't execute without contractors. The disbursement pipeline gap is a fintech product gap.

Key
Takeaway
Vietnam's infrastructure budget exists — the product that unlocks it (e-procurement rails + contractor finance) doesn't.
