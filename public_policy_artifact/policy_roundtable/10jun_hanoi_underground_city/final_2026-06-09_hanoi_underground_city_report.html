6Operator Insight

Sa. Partners view: Hanoi's underground city should not be analyzed as an infrastructure project. It is a legislative sprint disguised as a construction program. Phase 1 spans 9 years with zero construction. The immediate risk isn't failure — it's that land development in central Hanoi freezes for a decade while delivering nothing. The wedge opportunity is in the enabling layers: geospatial data platform, subsurface rights framework, and municipal bond market creation.
