03 — The Three Missing Layers (Each a Structural Blocker)
01

Property Rights

Vietnam's 2024 Land Law defines surface rights only. Below-ground ownership = legal void. No investor commits capital without depth-based ownership clarity.

02

Financing Mechanism

Municipal bonds outstanding: <$500M. Program capex est: $15–50B. No land value capture legislation. The mechanism to pay for this does not exist.

03

Subsurface Data

No comprehensive underground mapping. No digitized utility data. Cannot build at 30m without knowing what's at 5m. First product is data, not concrete.

05 — The Operator Insight
Phase 1 = 9 years of "legal framework." No construction. The immediate risk isn't failure — it's that land development in central Hanoi freezes for a decade while delivering nothing. Every developer, investor, property holder faces uncertainty today.
06 — The Opportunity Map
Builders

Wedge product = subsurface data platform. First mover on geospatial layer captures data moat for 40yr of construction. The product is a digital twin, not a tunnel.

Capital

Municipal infrastructure bonds = new SEA asset class. LVC legislation as catalyst. $15–50B financing gap is the investment thesis for infrastructure finance instruments.

Policy

Legislative sprint in Phase 1 determines if Phase 2 happens. Subsurface rights + LVC framework + capacity assessment — all needed in 9 years. Clock starts now.
